# PURSUE Release 01 — ETH Sub-Theory Analysis

**Derived from:** pursue_release01_dataset.csv (721 incidents, 36 fields, 1917–2025)
**Parent hypothesis:** One or more non-human civilizations from another star system operating vehicles in Earth's atmosphere
**Probability of parent hypothesis:** MEDIUM-HIGH for the irreducible anomalous core (18 cases)
**Method:** Each sub-theory tested against specific incident IDs and quantified patterns in the dataset

---

## Sub-Theory 1: Single Civilization, Long-Duration Survey Mission

One extraterrestrial civilization conducting a continuous multi-decade survey of Earth, analogous to long-baseline exoplanet observation with periodic close approaches.

**Predicted data signature:** Morphological consistency (same vehicles across eras). Behavioral consistency (same mission profile). Geographic patterns suggesting systematic coverage. Gradual activity increase.

**Actual data:** Morphological *inconsistency* — disc 53% in 1940s, sphere 25% in 2020s. Behavioral *evolution* — display/demonstration in 1940s–50s, transit/surveillance in 2020s. Geographic *fixation* on nuclear/military sites rather than systematic global coverage. Activity in sharp waves, not gradual increase.

**Verdict: WEAK.** The disc-to-sphere morphological shift is the primary problem. A single civilization running a standardized survey would not change vehicle morphology mid-program.

**Survival condition:** If the morphological change reflects sensor-dependent observation rather than actual change (i.e., a single object type presents as disc visually and sphere on IR), this sub-theory survives. Testable if Release 02 contains cases where the same object appears disc-shaped visually and spherical on IR simultaneously.

## Sub-Theory 2: Single Civilization, Phased Contact Program

One civilization executing a deliberate multi-stage contact program: observation (1940s) → demonstration (1950s–60s) → monitored self-disclosure (2020s).

**Predicted data signature:** Behavioral escalation across eras. Increased activity correlated with human technological milestones. The PURSUE release itself as a facilitated disclosure event.

**Actual data:** Behavioral progression IS present. 1947: distant visual observations. 1950: radar-confirmed facility surveillance (R01-0357/0358/0360 Oak Ridge). 1966: close approaches and vehicle interference (R01-0155 86-mile police chase, R01-0214 Daniels Park EM effects). 2020s: objects approaching within 10 feet of aircraft (R01-0062 Western US event), apparently pursuing military vehicles. Nuclear-proximity pattern correlates with weapons development milestones.

**Verdict: MODERATE.** Behavioral escalation is real in the data. COMETA explicitly theorized this: "Rather than observe us, it seems that they want to show themselves to us and to gradually acclimate us to the idea of their existence." Problem: unfalsifiability — any long-duration presence can be post-hoc interpreted as "phased contact."

**Confirmation test:** Release 02 contains cases with structured communication attempts — signals, patterns, repeated behaviors directed at observers interpretable as intentional communication. If escalation is deliberate, the next phase should include something beyond proximity.

## Sub-Theory 3: Multiple Civilizations, Independent Operations

Multiple extraterrestrial civilizations operating independently in Earth's atmosphere, each with different vehicles, behaviors, and objectives.

**Predicted data signature:** Multiple distinct morphological types coexisting. Distinct behavioral profiles mapping to distinct vehicle types. Geographic concentrations differing by type. Potentially competitive behaviors between types.

**Actual data:** Five distinct behavioral profiles documented: (1) surveillance/transit — CENTCOM sphere cases, straight-line flight, no interaction; (2) display/demonstration — 1948 Ohio cluster, shape-changing, hovering 35 minutes; (3) reactive/evasive — Andrews AFB 1948, responded to pursuit, evaded when illuminated; (4) aggressive/testing — Western US orbs, approached helicopter, pursued military aircraft, split into objects; (5) monitoring — Oak Ridge circling 3 days, White Sands missile-following. Multiple morphologies coexist within eras (disc AND sphere AND cigar AND triangle in 1950s at different ratios). Western US event shows multiple object types in a single incident.

**Verdict: STRONG.** Best explanation for the morphological diversity, the behavioral diversity, and the within-incident type variation. The disc-to-sphere shift could reflect one civilization's vehicles being replaced by another's. Five behavioral profiles map more naturally to five programs than one program with five modes.

**Prediction:** Release 02 should show incidents where different UAP types interact with each other — not just with human aircraft. The Western US "mother orb" launching smaller orbs may already show this.

## Sub-Theory 4: Single Civilization, Automated Probes

The objects are not crewed vehicles but autonomous probes — Von Neumann-type self-replicating machines or purpose-built sensor platforms deployed by a distant civilization that may or may not still exist.

**Predicted data signature:** No biological occupants. Behavior consistent with programmed responses rather than real-time decisions. Morphological variation as probes optimized for different environments. No communication (no one to communicate with). EM interaction as data collection rather than defense.

**Actual data:** "Benign" assessments and transit behaviors (straight-line FMV transits) are consistent with automated survey routines. EM effects could be probe sensors interacting with human electronics during data collection. No communication fits — probes don't talk. Morphological evolution could reflect probe generations (Model A disc, Model B sphere).

Problem: Western US event shows reactive/adaptive behavior (orbs pursuing helicopter, splitting, approaching within 10 ft). Andrews AFB pursuit shows tactical evasion with apparent awareness. These suggest real-time intelligence, not pre-programmed routines — unless AI is very sophisticated.

Counter-problem: Crash/recovery claims (R01-0356 Hottel memo: "three bodies of human shape but only three feet tall") directly contradict unmanned probes if occupants are real.

**Verdict: MODERATE.** Explains the lack of contact, morphological variation, and most surveillance behavior. Struggles with reactive cases but doesn't fail if probes have advanced AI.

**Denial test:** Confirmed biological occupants in any recovered vehicle.

## Sub-Theory 5: Single Civilization, Forward Operating Base

One civilization has established a persistent local presence — base(s) within the solar system (asteroid belt, lunar surface, ocean floor) — from which it operates atmospheric vehicles. Home civilization may be interstellar, but operational presence is local.

**Predicted data signature:** Sustained presence across decades. Response times consistent with in-system transit rather than interstellar travel. Return to specific geographic areas. Possible USO cases if oceanic basing. Lunar/orbital observations if near-Earth basing.

**Actual data:** 78-year continuous presence more consistent with local base than repeated interstellar trips. Skylab 1/3 red satellite (unidentified object in near-identical orbit, 30–50 NM away, rotating with 10-second period, never identified, never seen again) could be transit from orbital facility. Apollo 17 "flasher" tracked 24 hours at 10–12 Earth diameters could be orbital asset. Multiple ocean-surface cases (R01-0024 90° turns over water, R01-0025 surface flight, R01-0020 sphere with water reflection) consistent with ocean-proximate basing.

COMETA explicitly speculated: "Wouldn't it be logical to think that these extraterrestrial civilizations have established stations, even colonies, in the asteroid belt and why not relay stations on the moon?"

**Verdict: MODERATE-STRONG.** Sustained multi-decade presence is hard to explain with interstellar commuting. Local basing (lunar, asteroidal, oceanic, or orbital) is more parsimonious. Skylab and Apollo space-based observations provide thin but real evidence of near-Earth orbital presence.

**Confirmation test:** Discovery of artificial structures on lunar surface, in asteroid surveys, or on ocean floor. Cases showing objects arriving from or departing toward a consistent celestial direction.

## Sub-Theory 6: Civilization Studying Human Nuclear Development Specifically

An extraterrestrial civilization with specific interest in nuclear technology — either as a monitored threshold in developing species, because nuclear detonations are detectable at interstellar distances, or because nuclear weapons threaten something they value.

**Predicted data signature:** Disproportionate concentration near nuclear facilities. Activity onset correlated with nuclear weapons development. Escalation correlated with nuclear testing cadence. Monitoring without interference as default.

**Actual data:** 28 nuclear-proximate incidents. 17 at Oak Ridge alone (R01-0357, 0358, 0360, 0401, 0403, 0404, 0406–0410, 0440, 0651). Activity onset 1947 — two years after Trinity/Hiroshima. 1947 wave geographic center (Pacific NW) maps to Hanford plutonium production. Geographic migration tracks nuclear center of gravity: Oak Ridge → White Sands → Kirtland → CENTCOM/Iran. White Sands objects followed missiles in flight (R01-0229, 0364). No intervention during nuclear crises (Cuban Missile Crisis, Able Archer) — monitoring without interference.

Three competing explanations for nuclear interest:
- **(a) Detection:** Nuclear detonations produce signals (neutrinos, EM pulse, atmospheric isotopes) detectable at interstellar distances. Trinity as Earth's announcement. Activity onset 2 years later consistent with nearby star detection.
- **(b) Threat assessment:** Nuclear weapons mark transition to potentially spacefaring/dangerous civilization. Monitoring is prudent.
- **(c) Contamination concern:** Nuclear activity threatens something valued locally — a resource, an installation, the biosphere. COMETA: "fears for the future of earth, a future threatened by risks of nuclear war."

**Verdict: STRONG — strongest single sub-theory in the dataset.** Nuclear correlation is not just qualitative; it's the primary geographic organizing principle of the entire 78-year dataset. Onset timing, facility-specific surveillance, missile-tracking behavior, and sustained monitoring posture all precisely match predictions.

## Sub-Theory 7: Civilization Harvesting a Resource

An extraterrestrial civilization present to access a resource (biological, geological, energetic, informational) that Earth provides. Human nuclear development is a concern because it threatens the resource or harvesting operation, not from interest in humanity.

**Predicted data signature:** Activity patterns correlating with something other than human events. Geographic concentrations not mapping to human infrastructure. Behavioral indifference to observers. Environmental sampling behavior.

**Actual data:** Mixed. Most geographic concentrations map to human infrastructure (nuclear sites, military operations). But ocean-surface cases (Gulf of Aden cluster, Mediterranean, Arabian Gulf) could indicate interest in oceanic resources — we fly ISR over oceans where they also operate, creating appearance of military-proximity correlation. "Benign" assessment default and transit behaviors consistent with indifference. Objects ignoring human aircraft entirely (straight transit, no reaction) look like resource-access operations.

**Verdict: LOW-MODERATE.** Possible but nuclear-proximity pattern is more parsimonious as monitoring-specific explanation. Requires identifying what resource concentrates both near nuclear facilities and over ocean surfaces.

## MOST LIKELY COMPOSITE

The sub-theories are not mutually exclusive. The dataset best supports a composite:

**Multiple civilizations (Sub-Theory 3)**, at least one with **a local base in the solar system (Sub-Theory 5)**, with primary interest in **human nuclear development (Sub-Theory 6)**, executing a long-duration program that may include **phased contact elements (Sub-Theory 2)**.

This composite simultaneously accounts for:
- Morphological diversity (multiple sources)
- Sustained 78-year presence (local basing)
- Nuclear geographic correlation (nuclear monitoring intent)
- Behavioral escalation across eras (phased program)
- Five distinct behavioral profiles (multiple actors, multiple intents)
