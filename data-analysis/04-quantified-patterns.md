## TEMPORAL DISTRIBUTION

### The Six Waves

The dataset reveals six distinct activity peaks separated by troughs. These are not smooth trends — they're sharp spikes:

**Wave 1: July 1947 (109 incidents in a single month)**
The largest single concentration in the entire 108-year dataset. 45% of all 1947 incidents occurred in July alone. The wave onset is abrupt (14 incidents in June, 109 in July) and the decline is steep (30 in August, 6 in September). Geographic center: Pacific Northwest (Oregon 28, Idaho 28, Washington 24, California 27). This is the Arnold wave — social contagion and genuine observation are inseparable at this resolution.

**Wave 2: 1950 (49 incidents, sustained across the year)**
Unlike the sharp 1947 spike, 1950 is a sustained plateau. Geographic center shifts to Oak Ridge, Tennessee (14 incidents) and Ohio (14 incidents). This wave coincides with the first multi-day radar-confirmed cases and the first crash/recovery claims in FBI files (Hottel memo, March 1950). The character changes from mass-civilian-sighting to targeted-military-investigation.

**Wave 3: August–October 1952 (10+ incidents per month)**
A secondary peak coinciding with the Washington D.C. "flap" of July 1952 (the famous Washington National Airport radar cases, which are NOT in this dataset but are contemporaneous). The FBI records from this period show intensified institutional engagement and the beginning of the debunking posture that would define the Blue Book era.

**Wave 4: November 1957 (16 incidents in a single month)**
A sharp spike centered on the Levelland, Texas vehicle-interference cases and the post-Sputnik anxiety period. EM effects (engine failures, radio interference) are concentrated in this wave — the November 1957 Danville, Illinois police radio case is here.

**Wave 5: January–April 1966 (53 incidents across four months)**
The second-largest wave. Geographic center: Michigan/Ohio. This is the Dexter/Hillsdale "swamp gas" wave that triggered Rep. Gerald Ford's congressional inquiry and the formation of the Condon Committee. The 86-mile Ohio police chase is here. EM effects (Daniels Park car engine cutout) are here. This wave directly caused the Condon Report and the closure of Blue Book.

**Wave 6: 2020–2024 (48 incidents across five years)**
The modern era. Not a sharp spike but a sustained reporting baseline enabled by institutionalized UAP reporting procedures. Geographic center: CENTCOM AOR (27 incidents). The character is fundamentally different from all previous waves — sensor-confirmed (IR, FMV, radar), formally structured (MISREP UAP fields), and routed through AARO.

### The Troughs

**1970–2015: 11 incidents across 45 years.** This is the "dark period" — not because nothing was happening, but because the institutional reporting infrastructure was dismantled (Blue Book closed 1969) and the FBI stopped collecting (last substantial FBI entry is 1974). The trough is an artifact of reporting cessation, not phenomenon cessation.

### What the Timeline Shows

Three structural observations:

**1. Wave frequency is accelerating.** 1947 → 1950 (3 years) → 1952 (2 years) → 1957 (5 years) → 1966 (9 years) → 2020 (54 years, but the gap is a reporting artifact). If the dark period is excluded, the cadence is approximately one major wave every 3–9 years.

**2. Each wave shifts geographic center.** Pacific NW (1947) → Oak Ridge/Ohio (1950) → Washington DC area (1952) → Texas/Southwest (1957) → Michigan/Ohio (1966) → CENTCOM (2020). The phenomenon — or the reporting of it — migrates.

**3. The observer population evolves.** 1947: civilians, police, pilots (visual only). 1950: radar operators, FBI agents, AEC personnel (radar + visual). 1966: police, military pilots (visual + some radar). 2020: ISR crews with IR/FMV/radar (multi-sensor). Each wave is documented by increasingly sophisticated observers with increasingly capable sensors.

The disc dominance erodes from 53% to 2% across the dataset. Spheres rise from 11% to 25%. "Light/fireball/glow" peaks at 35% in the 1960s. The 2020s "other" category (64%) reflects the structured military descriptions that don't fit clean morphological categories ("glowing hot sphere with cylindrical pole/bar," "round diamond with non-maneuvering probe," "misshapen ball of white light classified as Plasma").

This is not noise. The disc-to-sphere transition tracks too cleanly across too many independent data sources (FBI, USAF, DOW) to be coincidental. Whether it reflects a real change in the phenomenon or a change in observation technology is the open question.
