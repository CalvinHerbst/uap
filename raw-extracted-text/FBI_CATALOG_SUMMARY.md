> Multiple credible witnesses including police officers, pilots, and military personnel reported sightings of fast-moving, disc-shaped objects over Portland Oregon and surrounding areas in early July 1947, with a contemporaneous Arizona sighting from 1917 also documented via photographs.

**Incident 1:** July 6, 1947 | Grafton, Wisconsin | Round metal disc about 18 inches in diameter, resembling a circular saw blade, approximately 1/8 inch thick, weighing about four or five pounds, still warm when found. Had a hole about one inch in diameter in the middle with gadgets, springs, and wires inside.
- Behavior: Found in yard after witness heard swishing, whirring noise followed by a thud and mild explosion. Later determined likely to be a circular saw, possibly thrown as a prank with a firecracker attached.
- Data: 18 inches diameter, 1/8 inch thick, 4-5 pounds weight, 1 inch hole in center, attached tubes about 3 inches long and 1 inch in diameter

**Incident 2:** July 1947 | Near St. Maries, Idaho | Saucer-shaped objects resembling washtubs, approximately the size of a five-room house

- Data: Fleet of 8 objects, 10 witnesses
